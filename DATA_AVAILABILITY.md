# Data availability and governance

The code repository contains no patient-level study data.

The ACRIN-NSCLC-FDG-PET collection can be requested or downloaded from
[The Cancer Imaging Archive](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/acrin-nsclc-fdg-pet/)
under the archive's current terms and citation requirements. Users are
responsible for obtaining all clinical tables, images, and ROI annotations from
authorized sources and for complying with the relevant data-use agreements.

The collection should be cited using its permanent record: Kinahan P, Muzi M,
Bialecki B, Herman B, and Coombs L. *Data from the ACRIN 6668 Trial
NSCLC-FDG-PET*. The Cancer Imaging Archive (2019).
[https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.2019.30ILQFCL](https://doi.org/10.7937/TCIA.2019.30ILQFCL).

Institution-specific clinical data, CT images, ROIs, extracted features,
survival outcomes, patient-level risk scores, and split manifests are not
redistributed. Access to non-TCIA cohorts is governed by the originating
institution, ethics approval, consent or waiver, and applicable privacy law.

The synthetic-data generator is provided solely to test installation and code
flow. Synthetic outputs are not evidence and must not be used to characterize
the study cohorts or models.
